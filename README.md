# DREAD — isometric sci-fi action RPG (Godot 4.7)

A "Doom meets Diablo" twin-stick action RPG: an exo-suited hunter carving through
candy-colored corrupted aliens in a dark industrial facility.

**Status: Phase 1 (Playable Combat Sandbox) is done.**
Phase 2 (procedural dungeon + arena lock-in) and Phase 3 (loot & mods) are next.

## Run it

1. Open the folder in **Godot 4.7.x** (Forward+ renderer).
2. Press **F5**. The main scene is `scenes/main.tscn`.

The first open imports the assets (a few seconds). No plugins needed.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| **WASD** | Move (camera-relative, 10 m/s, near-instant accel) |
| **Mouse** | Aim. Hover near an enemy and pink lock brackets show what your shots will hit (aim assist + bullet magnetism) |
| **LMB** | Fire (spends **Energy**) |
| **RMB / F** | Energy blade: a **3-hit combo** (mash it). Every hit **refunds Energy**, swings **reflect** bile globs, and a press next to a glowing (staggered or low-HP) enemy does a **finisher**. Slash during a dash for a dash-strike |
| **Space / Shift** | Dash (3 charges, invulnerable, passes through enemies, buffered) |
| **Q** | Micro-Rocket Swarm (homing salvo onto the enemies nearest your cursor) |
| **E** | Arc Nova (radial shockwave: knockback, stagger, clears projectiles) |
| **1 / 2, wheel** | Pulse Rifle / Plasma Scattergun |
| **Esc** | Pause · **R** Restart |

Gamepad: left stick moves, right stick aims, RT fires, RB uses the blade, A or LT dashes, LB is Q, Y is E.

**Debug keys:** F1 spawns a swarm, F2 a spitter, F3 a hulk. F5 toggles god mode, F6 kills everything, F7 toggles slow-mo.

**Settings:** F8 cycles graphics quality (LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH, saved; MEDIUM is the default). F9 toggles the FPS readout. M toggles music.
If the game ever feels sluggish, check the FPS readout first. Low framerate ruins game feel faster than anything else, and LOW mode is much lighter.

### The core loop

Energy is your ammo and it does not regenerate. You get it back by getting close and
using the blade: each cleave hit refunds energy. Enemies that are **staggered** or at
**low HP** glow orange with a spinning diamond overhead. Hit one with the blade to
**FINISH** it. You lunge in, slow-mo kicks in, and it bursts into health orbs.
Playing aggressively keeps you alive.

## Enemies (from the concept sheets)

| Archetype | Name | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| Swarmer | **Hatch Runner** | Fast pack hunter. It flanks to surround you, shows a red **line telegraph**, then lunges and bites. |
| Spitter | **Bloom Spitter** | Keeps medium range and strafes. Its bile sac swells and a **lime aim line** appears, then it fires a fan of slow corrosive globs that leave acid pools. You can reflect the globs with the blade. |
| Brute | **Tusk Hulk** | Heavy elite. Its **ground slam** has a filling red circle. If you kite it, it **leaps** onto your position. Below 35% HP its **core is exposed**: break its poise, then FINISH it. |

## Automated checks

```bash
godot --headless --path . --fixed-fps 60 -- --smoke-test
```

This runs 38 gameplay assertions and exits non-zero on any failure. It covers:

- movement speed and the 3 dash charges;
- the rifle killing small enemies, and **aim-assist accuracy on a strafing enemy with a deliberately sloppy cursor**;
- scattergun one-shots and the full 3-hit combo chain;
- the blade interrupting wind-ups, reflect, and brute stagger → finisher → health orbs;
- all three AI telegraph → attack loops, both abilities, and a 30-second wave soak with an autopilot bot.

Other user args (after `--`):

- `--autopilot` hands control to the bot with god mode on, for demos and soak tests. Use `--autopilot=melee` for a blade-focused bot.
- `--showcase` lines up the player and one of each enemy, idle. Use it to inspect art after swapping models.
- `--perf-probe` spawns a 48-enemy horde with the bot fighting it and prints game-logic frame times. Run it headless.
- `--screenshot=out.png --shot-delay=6` saves a frame and quits.

## Roadmap

- **Phase 2: procedural dungeon and arena lock-in.** A grid-based generator builds a start room, 3–5 combat arenas, corridors and a boss room. Entering an arena raises laser barriers (the sealed door in the sandbox previews this), runs waves through `WaveDirector.spawn_enemy()`, and drops loot when cleared. The navmesh is already baked at runtime.
- **Phase 3: loot and mods.** Glowing loot pillars, and mods that change behaviour. The hooks already exist: `WeaponController.shot_modifiers` (Split Shot), `projectile_hit_hooks` (Ricochet), `MeleeController.hit_hooks` (Vampiric Melee) and the `Player.dashed` signal (Explosive Dash).
